Registered Nurse - Medical/Surgical Unit

About us
Riverside Health has been caring for our community for over 60 years. Our 300-bed hospital is a Magnet-designated facility.

Responsibilities
- Provide direct patient care for 4-6 adult patients per shift on a busy med/surg floor
- Perform patient assessments, administer medications, and monitor vital signs
- Develop and update individualized care plans with physicians and case managers
- Educate patients and families on discharge instructions and medication management
- Document care accurately in the electronic health record (Epic)

Qualifications
- Active Registered Nurse (RN) license in the state of Pennsylvania
- Current BLS certification; ACLS required within 6 months of hire
- Minimum of two (2) years of acute care nursing experience
- Associate degree in Nursing required; BSN preferred
- Ability to work 12-hour shifts, including nights, weekends, and holidays

Preferred
- Telemetry or step-down experience
- Charge nurse experience

What we offer
Sign-on bonus, tuition reimbursement, and shift differentials.
